# Changelog

## 0.2.1
- After a red flag the race restarts from the grid for everyone, the pit lane starter too (before, you were sent to the pit lane again).

## 0.2.0
- Works with the F1 25 game update of 16 November 2026 (before, a pit lane start could leave the car stuck in its garage).
- New pit lane start: the game drives your car from the garage down the pit lane and it rolls gently to a stop at the pit exit.
- The start lights wait until you stand at the pit exit, so you watch the start from there.
- You stay held at the pit exit through the start; it opens a few seconds after the last car has passed.
- Your car keeps its gear on the way down the pit lane with the clutch out, so the engine drives it instead of revving on the limiter. The clutch prompt only shows once you stand at the line.
- On a track where the pit exit isn't known, the old garage start is used.
- One message when you break parc ferme (which rows you changed), instead of two.

## 0.1.3
- Tells Improved Car Setups (0.1.0 or newer) which setup rows are locked, so its setup screen can mark them.

## 0.1.2
- Cheaper memory checks with Swofty Launcher 1.9.30 or newer (fewer small hitches).

## 0.1.1
- Shows as tested on Steam in the launcher's Addon Browser, in the Utility category. Needs launcher 1.8.36 or newer.

## 0.1.0
- First release.
- The setup stays open after qualifying; changing a locked row means a pit lane start.
- Parc fermé starts when you first leave the pits in qualifying, and starts over when qualifying is restarted.
- A pit lane start begins in your garage: you drive out once the field has passed the pit exit.
- The pit lane start doesn't count as a pit stop.
- Parc fermé is kept if you quit the game between qualifying and the race, or quit the race and load it again.
- With Parc Fermé Rules off in the game's settings, the addon does nothing.
