# Changelog

## 0.1.2
- Cheaper memory checks with Swofty Launcher 1.9.30 or newer (fewer small hitches).

## 0.1.1
- Shows as tested on Steam in the launcher's Addon Browser, in the Utility category. Needs launcher 1.8.36 or newer.

## 0.1.0
- First release.
- The setup stays open after qualifying; changing a locked row means a pit lane start.
- Parc fermé starts when you first leave the pits in qualifying, and starts over when qualifying is restarted.
- A pit lane start begins in your garage: you drive out once the field has passed the pit exit.
- The pit lane start doesn't count as a pit stop.
- Parc fermé is kept if you quit the game between qualifying and the race, or quit the race and load it again.
- With Parc Fermé Rules off in the game's settings, the addon does nothing.
