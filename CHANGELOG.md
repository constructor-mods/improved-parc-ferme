# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First release.
- The setup stays open after qualifying; changing a locked row means a pit lane start.
- Parc fermé starts when you first leave the pits in qualifying, and starts over when qualifying is restarted.
- You wait at the end of the pit lane and go once the field has passed the pit exit, like a real pit lane start.
- The end of the pit lane is learned per track and kept; on a track it doesn't know yet, you start from the garage.
- The pit lane start doesn't count as a pit stop.
- Parc fermé is kept if you quit the game between qualifying and the race.
