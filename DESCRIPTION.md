Parc fermé the way it works in real F1. Got your setup wrong in qualifying? You can still fix it before the race, but it costs you: you start from the **pit lane**.

### How it works
- Parc fermé starts the moment you first leave the pits in **qualifying**.
- After that the **rear wing**, **suspension geometry**, **suspension**, **brake pressure** and **ballast** are locked by the rules.
- The setup screen doesn't grey them out any more. Change one and a message tells you which rows you changed.
- At the race start the game drives your car from its **garage** down the pit lane and stops it at the **pit exit**. The start lights wait until you stand there.
- You stay held at the pit exit while the field starts. A few seconds after the last car has passed, the pit exit opens and you join the race.
- On a track where the pit exit isn't known, your car waits in its garage instead and drives out once the field has passed.
- The pit lane start doesn't count as your pit stop.

### Still free
- Front wing, differential, brake bias, tyre pressures and fuel, like the real rule.

### Restarts
- Restart qualifying and parc fermé starts over.
- Restart the race and the pit lane start stays.
- Quit the game between qualifying and the race, or quit the race and load it again, and parc fermé is still there.

### Works with
- **Improved Car Setups** (0.1.0 or newer): its setup screen marks the locked rows and warns before you break parc fermé.

### Needs
- Swofty Launcher 1.8.36 or newer.
- A Grand Prix with qualifying on (Weekend Structure → Qualifying Format).
- Parc Fermé Rules on (Rules & Flags, on by default). With them off the addon does nothing.
- Offline sessions only, like all modded play.
