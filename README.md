# Improved Parc Fermé

Parc fermé like the real rule. After qualifying the setup isn't greyed out any more: you can still change it, but
changing a locked row means you start the race from the pit lane.

- **Locked once you leave the pits in qualifying:** rear wing, suspension geometry, suspension, brake pressure and
  ballast. Front wing, differential, brake bias, tyre pressures and fuel stay free.
- **Changed one?** A message says which rows, and you start the race from the pit lane: your car waits at the end of
  the pit lane and goes once the field has passed the pit exit.
- **The end of the pit lane** is learned the first time a car drives out of the pits on a track, and kept for the next
  visit. Until then the car starts from its garage and drives out once the field has gone by.
- **Restart qualifying** and parc fermé starts over.

Works in the offline Grand Prix with qualifying on. Offline only.
